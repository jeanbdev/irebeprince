Born of the First Sun

I am Irebe Manzi Jean D'Amour Prince, a professional Rwandan traditional dancer, born on September 14th — the day my elders named "the first sun." From that moment, I was recognized as a chosen keeper of the Intore dance, a warrior's discipline that demands not just movement, but ancestral fire.

Intore — meaning "the chosen one" — is more than performance: it is storytelling through leaps, chest-forward power, and rhythmic precision. I have dedicated my life to preserving its purity while projecting its majesty onto global stages.

My collaborations with @Impangazi have refined my agility and warrior pride. Every step echoes centuries of Rwandan royalty, yet speaks the universal language of excellence.
